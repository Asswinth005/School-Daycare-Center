Quick FAQ

What ages do you serve?

We welcome children ages 2-6 years old in our Toddler, Preschool, and Kindergarten programs.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?

Our ratios are 1:4 for Toddlers, 1:8 for Preschool, and 1:10 for Kindergarten.

Do you offer extended care?

Yes! We offer extended care from 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM, with flexible options.

Is there a registration fee?

A one-time registration fee of $150 is required to secure your child's spot.
