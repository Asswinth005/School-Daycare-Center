Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about enrollment, curriculum, and daily routines at Little Blossom.

What is your teaching philosophy?
We follow a child-led, play-based educational philosophy inspired by Reggio Emilia. We believe children learn best through active exploration, physical play, and guided curiosity in a warm, supportive community.
What are your student-to-teacher ratios?
We maintain high supervision ratios to guarantee personalized care: 1:4 for Toddlers (Ages 2-3), 1:8 for Preschoolers (Ages 4-5), and 1:10 for Kindergarten classes (Ages 5-6).
How do you handle dietary allergies and meals?
All meals and snacks are prepared fresh in our organic, nut-free campus kitchen. We strictly manage individualized allergy action plans and accommodate all medical dietary restrictions.
Can parents track their child's daily schedule?
Yes! Through our private and secure Parent Portal, you receive real-time updates, food logs, nap tracking, photos of school projects, and messages from teachers throughout the day.
Do you offer flexible part-time enrollments?
Yes, we offer both part-time (3 days per week) and full-time (5 days per week) schedules to accommodate the diverse scheduling needs of our families.
